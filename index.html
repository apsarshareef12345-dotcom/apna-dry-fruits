Five ways to buy dry fruits for less

Buy 1 kg packs

The per-kg price is the same on every size here, but fewer orders mean less delivery hassle and you never run out.

Compare per-kg, not per-pack

A small pack can look cheap and cost more per kg. Always divide by the weight before you decide.

Stock up after harvest

Nuts are freshest and often cheapest soon after harvest. Festive weeks are when prices climb.

Skip fancy gift boxes

You pay extra for the box. A plain pack with the same dry fruits is the better deal. Ask us for a simple gift pack on WhatsApp.

Store them well

Keep dry fruits in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place. Nuts stay crisp and last longer.
